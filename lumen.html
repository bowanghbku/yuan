LUMEN

Bend beams of light with mirrors until every crystal glows. Tap an empty square to place a mirror, tap again to flip it, and tap a third time to pick it up.

Each level gives you exactly the mirrors you need. New pieces appear as you go: locked mirrors, dark crystals that must stay dark, coloured light, colour filters and beam splitters.
