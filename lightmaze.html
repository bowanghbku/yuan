Lightmaze

You're a firefly lost in a dark maze. Your glow only lights the corridors close by. Find the three light keys to open the exit door, then fly out.

Shadows drift through the maze and chase you when they get close. Touch one and you lose a heart. Three hearts per run. Every level is a new, bigger maze with more shadows.

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Hold a key to keep going.

Swipe in a direction and the firefly flies that way until it hits a wall or a turn.
