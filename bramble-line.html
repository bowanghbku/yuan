Bramble Line

Clockwork beetles are marching on your seed vault. Grow defenders on the garden beds to stop them. The vault can survive three beetles getting through; the fourth one wins.

Dew Lily 50: makes dew, your currency Thorn Spitter 100: fires thorns down its row Bramble Wall 50: tough blocker Frost Fern 175: ice shards slow beetles Burr Bomb 150: blows up a 3×3 patch Trowel: dig up a plant

Pick a card, then tap a bed tile to plant it. Tap the glowing dew drops to collect them (they also drift into your jar on their own after a few seconds).

Keys: 1–5 pick a plant, Q trowel, Esc cancel, P pause.
