Level 0 · goal 80 m

Each level has a goal depth. Fly through the green goal gate to unlock the next level, which starts again from the cave entrance with a tighter cave, fewer cushions, more spikes and a hungrier battery.

Watch out for cars driving along the cave floor (one red one is very fast) and other drones flying around: touch either one and you're out. Air plants on the floor blow a jet of air that pushes your drone up, but touching the plant itself ends the run. Water taps on the ceiling pour a stream that pushes your drone down; the taps themselves are solid. Some stretches of the ceiling and floor have blue cushions: you bounce off them, but each bounce costs a little battery. Bare amber rock and red spikes are hard: hit them and it's over. The battery drains as you fly, and thrust drains it faster. Grab the green cells to recharge.
