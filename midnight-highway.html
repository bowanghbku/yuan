Midnight Highway

Race down the night highway as far as you can. Your car speeds up on its own; weave around the traffic, stay on the road, and reach each checkpoint for more time.

Hitting a car or driving on the grass slows you down. When the clock reaches zero, the drive is over.

Steer with ← → or A D. Brake with ↓ or S.

Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer.
