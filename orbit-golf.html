Orbit Golf

Mini golf in space. Drag back from your comet ball and let go to fling it, like a slingshot. The further you pull, the harder the shot.

Planets pull on the ball, bending its path, so you can curve shots around them. Ball resting on a planet? Shoot from there. Sink it in the purple wormhole.

Black holes swallow the ball: +1 penalty stroke and you shoot again from where you were. 12 holes; try to finish under par.
