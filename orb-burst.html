Orb Burst

Aim with your mouse or finger and release (or click) to fire an orb. Orbs bounce off the side walls.

Join three or more of the same colour to pop them. Orbs left hanging with nothing holding them up drop for bonus points.

Every few shots that don't pop anything, the whole cluster creeps down a row. Clear the board to win the level; let it reach the line and it's over. Tap the waiting orb to swap it with the next one.
