Glowtail

You're a little glowing creature gliding across a night pond. Swallow the sparks to grow your tail. Golden sparks are worth 5 and give a speed burst.

Don't touch the pond's edge, the stones that appear as you grow, or your own tail.

Steer with ← → (or A D), or just point with the mouse.

Steer by holding your finger where you want to go.
