You are a Firefly

Moonmeadow

A quiet night meadow by a lake. You start as a tiny firefly, but you can become anything nearby: a fox, an owl, a fish, a tree, even a rock or a cloud.

Each thing moves its own way. Fish can only swim, deer can't cross water, trees and rocks stand still but can reach further to jump into something else. Fill your journal by being all 16 things.

Move with arrow keys or WASD. Become something glowing near you by clicking it, or press Space for the closest one.

Tap the ground to move there. Tap something glowing near you to become it.

You've been everything

Firefly to cloud, fox to fish: you've been all 16 things in Moonmeadow. Keep wandering as long as you like.
