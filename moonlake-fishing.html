Moonlake Fishing

One night on a glowing lake. Catch as much as you can before dawn, about two minutes.

Cast: press and hold, then let go when the power meter is where you want it. Your lure sinks slowly; hold to reel it back in and draw fish toward it.

Bite! When you see the !, tap fast to hook the fish. Then hold to reel, but watch the tension bar: let go when it turns red or the line snaps, and don't leave it slack for too long or the fish slips away.

8 kinds of fish live at different depths. Some are rare.
