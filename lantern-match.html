Lantern Match

Swap two neighbouring lanterns (drag one onto the other, or tap them one after the other) to line up three or more of the same shape. They pop, new lanterns drop in, and chain reactions score extra.

Match 4 to make a striped lantern that clears a whole row or column when matched. Match 5 to make a prism: swap it with any lantern to clear every lantern of that kind.

Reach the goal score before you run out of moves. Score higher for up to 3 stars.
